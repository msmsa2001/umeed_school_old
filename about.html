About Ummeed Foundation

Our journey to building an inclusive society for every child.

Our History

Founded on Hope and Necessity

Ummeed Foundation was founded in 2011 by Mr. Parvez Farid. The inspiration behind Ummeed was born out of a personal struggle to find proper, specialized education and therapy for his nephew who was diagnosed with special needs.

Realizing the severe lack of accessible, personalized care and education for children with disabilities in Mumbra and surrounding regions, he took the initiative to build a space where every child could receive the attention they deserve.

Since then, Ummeed has grown from a small initiative into a comprehensive rehabilitation and education center, transforming the lives of hundreds of children and their families.

Our Mission

To provide personalized education, comprehensive therapies, and vocational training for children with disabilities, ensuring they reach their fullest potential and achieve independence in their daily lives.

Our Vision

To build a truly inclusive society where every child, regardless of their physical or cognitive abilities, has an equal opportunity to learn, grow, and contribute meaningfully to the world.
