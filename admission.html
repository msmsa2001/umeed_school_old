Student Admissions

Welcoming your child into our caring community.

We believe in a thorough, compassionate admission process to ensure that we are perfectly equipped to support your child's specific developmental needs.

1

Initial Inquiry

Contact us via phone or our website to schedule a preliminary visit to our center and discuss your child's background.

2

Clinical Assessment

Our team of therapists and special educators will conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your child's cognitive, physical, and sensory abilities.

3

Parent Counseling

We review the assessment results with you, discussing realistically what therapies are needed and how the personalized intervention plan will look.

4

Enrollment

Upon mutual agreement of the intervention plan, you can complete the registration paperwork and your child's journey with Ummeed begins.
