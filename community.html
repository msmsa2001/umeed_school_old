Community Outreach

Fostering awareness, access, and assistance across society.

We are committed to creating a positive impact beyond our classrooms. Our Community Engagement initiatives are designed to foster collaboration, raise awareness, and provide essential support to those in need.

Government Schemes

  • Description: We assist families in navigating and accessing various government schemes and benefits tailored for children with disabilities. This support ensures that families can make the most of available resources and opportunities.
  • Features: Guidance on application processes, assistance with paperwork, information on available schemes.

Medical Aids Drive

  • Description: Organizing drives to collect and distribute medical aids such as hearing aids, wheelchairs, and other essential equipment to those in need. This initiative aims to enhance the quality of life for individuals with disabilities in our community.
  • Features: Equipment collection, distribution events, partnership with local organizations.

PWD Awareness Programs

  • Description: Programs aimed at raising awareness about disabilities and promoting inclusion within the community. These initiatives include workshops, seminars, and public campaigns to educate and encourage understanding and empathy.
  • Features: Educational sessions, community events, collaboration with local media.
