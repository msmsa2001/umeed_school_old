Corporate Partnerships

Driving sustainable impact through strategic CSR alignment.

Aligning With Global Goals

Ummeed Foundation presents a unique opportunity for corporate partners to fulfill their Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) mandates while making a targeted, measurable impact on the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

3

Good Health & Well-being

Supporting our therapies, medical camps, and rehabilitation services directly contributes to the mental and physical well-being of children with disabilities.

4

Quality Education

Funding our special education programs ensures that children with cognitive challenges receive an education tailored to their needs.

10

Reduced Inequalities

Our entire mission is built on creating an inclusive society. Partnering with us helps tear down systemic barriers faced by PWDs.
