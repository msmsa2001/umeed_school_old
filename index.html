Our Founding Story

Born from a Father's
Determination & Love

In 2010, Mr. Parvez Farid searched tirelessly for a school to admit his nephew Salman, who was born with a mental disorder. Every door in Mumbai was closed. The only options were a distant government school or private schools with years-long waiting lists.

Parvez began educating Salman himself, and soon realized hundreds of other families faced the same heartbreak. This became the spark that lit Ummeed.

Starting in rented rooms in 2011, Ummeed has grown into a full-fledged school operating in premises allotted by the Thane Municipal Corporation.
