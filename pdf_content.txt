At Ummeed The Hope Special School, we welcome every child with open arms and open hearts. 
For over 13 years, our school run by Ummeed Foundation has been a home where children with

About Us 
Introduction 
Ummeed The Hope Special School, an initiative of the Ummeed Foundatio n, has been a beacon 
of hope and support for children with special needs since its inception. Established in 2011, our 
school has been dedicated to providing personalized education and care for children facing 
various challenges. 
Our History 
The story of Ummeed began with Mr. Parvez Farid, who faced a deeply personal challenge. In 
2010, Parvez was trying to find a suitable school for his nephew, Salman, who was born with a 
mental disorder. Despite numerous attempts, no school in Mumbai was willing to admit  Salman. 
The only options available were a government school in Thane and private schools with long 
waiting periods. This struggle for Salman was a wake-up call for Parvez. 
In the face of this unnerving challenge, Parvez decided to take matters into his ow n hands. He 
began educating Salman himself and soon realized that many other children like Salman were in 
similar situations. Driven by a passion to make a difference, Parvez founded Ummeed, meaning 
'hope,' with the vision to provide quality education and support for children with special needs. 
Initially starting by renting rooms, Ummeed The Hope Special School has grown significantly over 
the years. We are now privileged to operate in premises allotted by the Thane Municipal 
Corporation, allowing us to extend our reach and services. 
Our Mission & Vision 
Our mission is to create a nurturing environment where every child with special needs receives the 
education, care, and support they deserve. We aim to: 
 Provide personalized education tailored to each child’s unique needs. 
 Enhance physical, social, and emotional development through comprehensive therapies.  
 Foster independence and self-reliance in daily activities. 
 Promote inclusivity and understanding within the community.

Services 
1. Special Education (Sub Page) 
 
At Ummeed The Hope Special School, our Special Education services focus on providing 
personalized and effective learning experiences for each child. Our approach is designed to 
meet diverse needs and foster both academic and personal growth.  
Personalized Learning Plans - IEP 
Individualized Education Plan (IEP) for each student, tailored to their unique abilities and needs. 
These plans include specific goals, teaching methods, and necessary resources, regularly 
reviewed to adapt to the child’s progress. 
Adaptive Teaching Methods 
Our teaching strategies are flexible, incorporating various methods such as visual aids, hands -on 
activities, and verbal instruction to match different learning styles. This ensures that every child 
engages effectively and reaches their potential. 
Assertive Learning Devices 
We incorporate assertive learning devices and tools to support individualized learning. These 
devices assist in reinforcing lessons and enhancing engagement, helping students achieve their 
learning goals more effectively. 
Inclusive Environment 
We create a supportive and inclusive classroom atmosphere where every child feels valued. 
Positive interactions, cooperative learning, and celebration of achievements are integral to our 
teaching philosophy. 
Parental Involvement 
Collaboration with parents is crucial. We maintain regular communication to discuss progress, 
address concerns, and adjust strategies, ensuring that parents are active partners in their child’s 
education. 
2. Remedial Intervention  
 
we understand that every child learns differently. Our remedial Intervention program is designed 
to provide targeted support to help children overcome academic challenges and achieve their 
full potential. 
 
One-on-One Support: We offer individual sessions to provide focused attention and addres s 
specific learning difficulties. This personalized approach helps build confidence and reinforces 
foundational skills. 
Progress Monitoring: Regular assessments track each child’s progress and adapt the learning plan 
as needed. This ensures that every child receives the support they need to succeed 
academically. 
Parent Involvement: We actively involve parents in the educational process, providing them with 
strategies and resources to support their child's learning at home.

1. Therapy’s (Sub Page) 
 
Physiotherapy  
 
Expert Guidance 
Dr. Ali Irani, a distinguished name in physiotherapy in India, leads our physiotherapy team. 
Renowned for his extensive academic and clinical experience, Dr. Irani has a celebrated career 
that includes serving as the physiotherapist for the Indian cricket team from 1987 to 1997. His 
expertise is sought after in both the sports world and among Bollywood celebrities, having assisted 
numerous athletes and celebrities in their recovery from serious injuries.  
Annual Assessments 
Each year, professional physiotherapists from Nanavati Hospital visit Ummeed The Hope Special 
School for a comprehensive one-month assessment. They evaluate our students and develop a 
detailed annual plan for each child, tailored to their individual needs an d progress. 
Family Collaboration 
We actively involve families in the therapy process, offering guidance and support to reinforce 
therapy goals at home. This partnership enhances the effectiveness of therapy and contributes to 
the child’s overall progress. 
Speech Therapy  
Our Approach: 
 Personalized Sessions: Tailored therapy plans address individual communication 
challenges, from articulation to language comprehension. 
 Expert Care: Our qualified therapists use advanced, evidence-based methods to 
enhance speech and language skills. Regular assessments ensure therapy plans remain 
effective and aligned with each child's progress. 
 Unique Offering: Within a 20 km radius, no other free speech therapy centers are 
available. Our program fills this crucial gap, providing vital support to children and families 
in need. 
Impactful Outcomes: 
Our speech therapy services empower children to express themselves confidently and engage 
fully with their peers and community. By using the latest equipment and techniques, we strive to 
make a meaningful difference in each child's life. 
Occupational Therapy (Sub Page) 
Key Features: 
 Customized Therapy: Individualized programs to enhance skills for daily activities, such as 
self-care and play. 
 Expert Therapists: Sessions led by skilled occupational therapists with extensive experience 
in child development. 
 Cutting-Edge Equipment: Use of the latest tools and technology to support therapy and 
track progress effectively. 
 Parent Involvement: Training and guidance for parents to reinforce therapy goals and 
strategies at home

2. Medical Aids (Sub Page) 
At Ummeed The Hope Special School, we are dedicated to enhancing the daily lives of our 
students through tailored medical aids. 
Essential Medical Equipment: We provide critical medical equipment such as hearing aids, CP 
chairs, callipers, limbs, and walkers. These aids are selected based on individual assessments to 
ensure that each child has access to the necessary tools for improved mobility and daily 
functioning. 
Collaborative Approach: In collaboration with the District Disability Rehabilitation Centre and 
government agencies, we ensure that the equipment provided meets the highest standards and 
addresses the specific needs of each student. 
Personalized Assessments: Our team conducts thorough assessments to determine the most 
appropriate aids for each child. This personalized approach helps in optimizing the effectiveness 
of the medical equipment. 
3. Vocational Training (Sub Page) 
Our vocational training program is designed to equip students with practical skills that foster 
independence and enhance employability. We offer a diverse range of training modules tailored 
to each student's abilities and interests. 
Comprehensive Skill Development: We provide hands-on training in various vocational areas, 
including: 
 Crafts and Handicrafts: Skills in weaving, pottery, and other craft-based activities. 
 Advance Computer Skills: Advance computer operations and software knowledge. 
 Retail Skills: Customer service, transaction handling, and basic inventory management. 
 Food Preparation: Training in making popular local foods such as wada pav, tea, cakes, 
and chocolates. 
 Automotive Care: Basic car and bike washing techniques. 
 Telecalling: Skills for effective communication and customer service in a telecalling 
environment. 
Personalized Training Plans: Each student receives a customized training plan based on their 
strengths and career interests, ensuring that the training is relevant and impactful.  
Industry Partnerships: We collaborate with local businesses and vocational centers to provide 
real-world experience and hands-on training. These partnerships help bridge the gap between 
education and employment. 
Job Readiness Skills: Our program includes training in essential soft skills such as communication, 
time management, and workplace etiquette, preparing students for the job market.  
Life Skills Integration: Alongside vocational skills, we focus on life skills training including financial 
literacy, personal hygiene, and daily living skills to promote overall independence. 
Post-Training Support: We offer guidance and support for job placement and career 
development, helping students transition smoothly from training to employment or further 
education.

The Advanced Computer Training Centre at Ummeed The Hope Special School is dedicated to

5. Documentation (Sub Page) 
Documentation system is crucial for tracking each child’s progress, providing tailored care, and 
ensuring that they receive the support they need. Our comprehensive approach to 
documentation ensures that every aspect of a child's development is recorded and managed 
effectively. 
Medical Records: We maintain detailed medical records that include assessments, treatments, 
and interventions. This helps ensure that healthcare needs are meticulously documented and 
monitored. 
Therapy Progress Reports: Progress reports for physiotherapy, speech therapy, and occupational 
therapy are maintained to track achievements, challenges, and adjustments needed in therapy 
plans. 
Aadhar Card, UDID (Disability Card) : We make all necessary documentation for government 
schemes and regulations, such as financial assistance applications, disability certifications, and 
other relevant paperwork. This includes documents like IQ certificates, disability certificates, and 
any other required proofs for Persons with Disabilities (PWD)

Ummeed Rehab Centre 
The Ummeed Rehab Centre is an essential extension of Ummeed The Hope Special School, 
dedicated to providing specialized rehabilitation services. Located in Mumbra, this centre 
supports children with a range of physical and developmental challenges, offering a 
comprehensive approach to their rehabilitation needs. 
Need 
Many children with disabilities face significant barriers to accessing adequate rehabilitation 
services. The Ummeed Rehab Centre addresses this gap by offering tailored programs that cater 
to each child's specific requirements. Our centre is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and 
staffed by experienced professionals to ensure effective rehabilitation.  
Currently there are 111+ Special Children, 50 Senior Citizens & 97 Women were benefiting through 
this cause 
Services on Weekly Basis  
 Physiotherapy: Focuses on enhancing physical strength, coordination, and mobility 
through individualized and group sessions. 
 Speech Therapy: Addresses speech and communication challenges, using the latest 
equipment and techniques. 
 Occupational Therapy: Helps improve daily living skills and functional abilities, using 
innovative therapeutic methods. 
 Medical Aids: Provides essential equipment like hearing aids, CP chairs, callipers, limbs, 
and walkers, based on individual assessments. 
 Remedial Intervention: Offers personalized educational support to address learning 
difficulties and academic challenges. 
 Psychological Assessment: Conducts thorough evaluations to develop personalized 
rehabilitation plans.

Student Success Stories 
Shoeb Arif Sarang 
Shoeb’s journey from a normal school to Ummeed The Hope Special School is a story of 
remarkable transformation. Initially having low vision and only able to read large prints, Shoeb’s 
vision deteriorated due to multiple eye surgeries, leading to complete blindness. Enrolling at  
Ummeed in 2011, Shoeb’s dedication and the support he received have been outstanding. 
Today, he serves as a Special Educator at Ummeed The Hope Special School, guiding and 
inspiring new students with his own experiences.

journey didn’t stop there—Tabish is now working as an IT Technician at the same school, 
contributing his skills and knowledge to support his peers and the institution.
