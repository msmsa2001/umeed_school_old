Specialized Programs

Empowering children, equipping parents, and educating teachers.

We believe that education extends beyond the classroom. Our Programs & Training focus on holistic development, equipping children with skills and knowledge to thrive in their personal and academic lives.

Programs for Children

Life Skills Training

Description: Practical skills training that covers essential life skills such as personal hygiene, money management, and basic cooking. This program aims to foster independence and self-reliance among students.

Features: Hands-on activities, real-life scenarios, skill assessments.

Social Skills Development

Description: Activities and workshops designed to improve social interactions, communication, and relationship-building skills. This program helps students develop confidence and navigate social situations effectively.

Features: Role-playing, group activities, social interaction exercises.

Parent Workshops

  • Description: Educational workshops for parents to enhance their understanding of their child’s needs and how to support their development effectively. Topics include behavior management, educational strategies, and emotional support.
  • Features: Expert-led sessions, interactive discussions, practical advice.

Teacher Training Programs

  • Description: Specialized training for teachers to equip them with the latest techniques and strategies for teaching children with special needs. This includes training on inclusive education practices, behavioral management, and innovative teaching methods.
  • Features: Workshops, hands-on practice, ongoing professional development.
