Late Mrs. S.K. Parvez Free Rehab Centre

The Late Mrs. S.K. Parvez Free Rehab Centre is a cornerstone of our community support. While special education builds cognitive foundations, deep physical and sensory interventions often require a dedicated, clinical environment. The Ummeed Rehab Centre was established to offer intensive, goal-oriented therapies. It bridges the gap between educational learning and everyday functional independence, making sure therapies are consistent, monitored, and highly personalized for complex cases.
