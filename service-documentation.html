Documentation Support

Ensuring that every aspect of a child's care and benefits is recorded and managed effectively.

A proper documentation system is crucial for tracking each child’s progress, providing tailored care, and ensuring that they receive the support they need. Our comprehensive approach ensures effectiveness.

Medical Records

We maintain detailed medical records that include assessments, treatments, and interventions. This helps ensure that healthcare needs are meticulously documented and monitored.

Therapy Progress Reports

Progress reports for physiotherapy, speech therapy, and occupational therapy are maintained to track achievements, challenges, and adjustments needed in therapy plans.

Aadhar Card & UDID (Disability Card)

We make all necessary documentation for government schemes and regulations, such as financial assistance applications, disability certifications, and other relevant paperwork. This includes documents like IQ certificates, disability certificates, and any other required proofs for Persons with Disabilities (PWD).
