Medical Aids

Enhancing the daily lives of our students through tailored medical aids.

At Ummeed The Hope Special School, we are dedicated to enhancing the daily lives of our students through tailored medical aids.

Essential Medical Equipment

We provide critical medical equipment such as hearing aids, CP chairs, callipers, limbs, and walkers. These aids are selected based on individual assessments to ensure that each child has access to the necessary tools for improved mobility and daily functioning.

Collaborative Approach

In collaboration with the District Disability Rehabilitation Centre and government agencies, we ensure that the equipment provided meets the highest standards and addresses the specific needs of each student.

Personalized Assessments

Our team conducts thorough assessments to determine the most appropriate aids for each child. This personalized approach helps in optimizing the effectiveness of the medical equipment.
