Our Therapies

Expert-led Physiotherapy, Speech Therapy, and Occupational Therapy.

Physiotherapy

Expert Guidance

Dr. Ali Irani, a distinguished name in physiotherapy in India, leads our physiotherapy team. Renowned for his extensive academic and clinical experience, Dr. Irani has a celebrated career that includes serving as the physiotherapist for the Indian cricket team from 1987 to 1997. His expertise is sought after in both the sports world and among Bollywood celebrities, having assisted numerous athletes and celebrities in their recovery from serious injuries.

Annual Assessments

Each year, professional physiotherapists from Nanavati Hospital visit Ummeed The Hope Special School for a comprehensive one-month assessment. They evaluate our students and develop a detailed annual plan for each child, tailored to their individual needs and progress.

Family Collaboration

We actively involve families in the therapy process, offering guidance and support to reinforce therapy goals at home. This partnership enhances the effectiveness of therapy and contributes to the child’s overall progress.

Speech Therapy

Our Approach:
  • Personalized Sessions: Tailored therapy plans address individual communication challenges, from articulation to language comprehension.
  • Expert Care: Our qualified therapists use advanced, evidence-based methods to enhance speech and language skills. Regular assessments ensure therapy plans remain effective and aligned with each child's progress.
  • Unique Offering: Within a 20 km radius, no other free speech therapy centers are available. Our program fills this crucial gap, providing vital support to children and families in need.
Impactful Outcomes:

Our speech therapy services empower children to express themselves confidently and engage fully with their peers and community. By using the latest equipment and techniques, we strive to make a meaningful difference in each child's life.

Occupational Therapy

Key Features:
  • Customized Therapy: Individualized programs to enhance skills for daily activities, such as self-care and play.
  • Expert Therapists: Sessions led by skilled occupational therapists with extensive experience in child development.
  • Cutting-Edge Equipment: Use of the latest tools and technology to support therapy and track progress effectively.
  • Parent Involvement: Training and guidance for parents to reinforce therapy goals and strategies at home.
