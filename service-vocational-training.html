Vocational Training

Equipping students with practical skills that foster independence and enhance employability.

Our vocational training program is designed to equip students with practical skills that foster independence and enhance employability. We offer a diverse range of training modules tailored to each student's abilities and interests.

Comprehensive Skill Development

We provide hands-on training in various vocational areas, including:

Crafts & Handicrafts

Skills in weaving, pottery, and other craft-based activities.

Advance Computer Skills

Advance computer operations and software knowledge.

Retail Skills

Customer service, transaction handling, and basic inventory management.

Food Preparation

Training in making popular local foods such as wada pav, tea, cakes, and chocolates.

Automotive Care

Basic car and bike washing techniques.

Telecalling

Skills for effective communication and customer service in a telecalling environment.

  • Personalized Training Plans

    Each student receives a customized training plan based on their strengths and career interests, ensuring that the training is relevant and impactful.

  • Industry Partnerships

    We collaborate with local businesses and vocational centers to provide real-world experience and hands-on training. These partnerships help bridge the gap between education and employment.

  • Job Readiness Skills

    Our program includes training in essential soft skills such as communication, time management, and workplace etiquette, preparing students for the job market.

  • Life Skills Integration

    Alongside vocational skills, we focus on life skills training including financial literacy, personal hygiene, and daily living skills to promote overall independence.

Post-Training Support

We offer guidance and support for job placement and career development, helping students transition smoothly from training to employment or further education.
