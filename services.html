Holistic Development

We follow a multi-disciplinary approach to ensure every child receives personalized attention in education, physical health, and independence.

Special Education (IEP)

Our Special Education focus is on personalized learning. We foster academic growth through Individualized Education Plans (IEP) tailored to unique abilities.

  • Adaptive Teaching Methods (IEP focus)
  • Assertive Learning Devices & Tools
  • Inclusive & Visual Classroom Aids
  • Regular Parent Collaboration & Updates

Clinical Therapies

Led by world-class experts, our therapy programs help children achieve physical and communication milestones using evidence-based methods.

  • Physiotherapy led by Dr. Ali Irani
  • Annual assessments by Nanavati Hospital
  • Only free Speech Therapy center in 20km
  • Professional Occupational therapy equipment
